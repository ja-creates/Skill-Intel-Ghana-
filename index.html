About Skill Intel Ghana

This tool helps Ghanaians discover practical, income-generating skills and side hustles that are realistic to start with low-to-medium capital. It provides step-by-step guides, profit estimators, and progress tracking so you can move from idea to action.

It is an educational and informational resource only. We do not provide financial, legal, or career advice.

Important Disclaimer

Skill Intel Ghana is provided for general informational and educational purposes only. We do not endorse, certify, recommend, or have any affiliation with any training institutions, government programmes, or third parties mentioned.

All information (skills, costs, income estimates, training suggestions) is illustrative and based on publicly available knowledge as of 2026. Actual results, costs, and opportunities vary by location, individual effort, market conditions, and official requirements.

Always verify directly with official sources (your District Assembly, NBSSI, MoFA, FDA, Energy Commission, etc.) before making any financial or business decisions. Use this tool at your own risk.

Privacy & Data

We collect nothing.

Skill Intel Ghana is a completely client-side tool. All your progress, reviews, ratings, points, and settings are stored only in your browser’s localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server, and we have no access to your data.

You can clear everything anytime from your browser settings or using the “Clear all data” button in Profile. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no third-party analytics.
